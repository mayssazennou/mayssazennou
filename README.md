## Hi, I'm Mayssa 👋

I'm drawn to the engineering behind software: the principles, architectures and methods that turn a complex problem into a reliable system.

I like to start by breaking things down, identifying the real pain points and tracing issues back to their cause. Troubleshooting is where I learn the most.

From there, I enjoy working across the whole chain: designing the architecture of a system, weighing trade-offs, choosing the right technologies, building the back end and interfaces around it, and taking it all the way to deployment.

I also like plugging in intelligent components (ML models, LLM-based agents) where they actually add value.

I also love building hackathon prototypes: turning an idea into a working product under time pressure, where quick decisions, clear priorities and teamwork matter as much as the code.

Get in touch: [My LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mayssa-zennou-217428341/)
