## Hi, I'm Mayssa 👋

Final-year **software engineering student** at **ENSIAS** (École Nationale Supérieure d'Informatique et d'Analyse des Systèmes, Morocco).

I'm passionate about the engineering behind software: the principles, architectures and methods that turn a complex problem into a reliable system. I like to start by understanding a situation: breaking it down, identifying the real pain points and tracing issues back to their cause. Troubleshooting is where I learn the most. From there, I enjoy working across the whole chain: designing the architecture of a system, weighing trade-offs, choosing the right technologies, building the back end and interfaces around it, taking it all the way to deployment, and plugging in intelligent components (ML models, LLM-based agents) where they actually add value.

## What I build

I build full-stack applications while exploring distributed systems, microservices and AI-powered applications in depth, learning by building, breaking and fixing things along the way.

I also love building hackathon prototypes: turning an idea into a working product under time pressure, where quick decisions, clear priorities and teamwork matter as much as the code.
